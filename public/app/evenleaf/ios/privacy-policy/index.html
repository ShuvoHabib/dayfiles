Your privacy in Ink & Echo

Effective November 28, 2026. This policy covers Ink & Echo for Android, iPhone and iPad.

Documents and reading history

Ink & Echo keeps your imported PDFs, extracted text, bookmarks, notes, reading position and per-document preferences in its private storage on your device. OCR and narration run locally. Choose Remove from library to delete the app’s copy and its reading data. Copies you share or export are handled by the destination you choose.

Optional usage analytics

Usage analytics are off until you enable them in Reader access and privacy. If enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase receives app interactions such as reader views, opening plans or starting checkout, together with app-instance and device information. These events exclude document names, content, annotations and reading positions. Turn off Share usage analytics in the same settings to stop future collection.

Version checks and voice downloads

Firebase Remote Config uses an installation identifier and app information to check whether a newer build is required. When you download the optional voice pack, the file host, Hugging Face, receives your download request and IP address. Your reading text stays on the device during narration.

Purchases, reminders and support

Google Play processes Android payments and Apple processes iOS payments. On Android, a local record remembers which PDF received the free import. Removing the PDF does not reset the allowance. The record stays on the device. Ink & Echo checks your eligible offers and purchase entitlement with the store. Optional reminders are scheduled on your device. If you email support, Dayfiles receives your email address and the information you choose to send to handle your request.

Future AI features

Monthly and lifetime access cover Ink & Echo’s reader features, including on-device text recognition and narration. Both plans exclude AI features introduced in the future. If we offer a paid AI feature, we’ll show its price and terms before you decide whether to buy it.

Privacy questions

Contact contact@dayfiles.com for questions or a request about information you sent to support.

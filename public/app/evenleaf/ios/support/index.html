Help with Ink & Echo

Open your PDF

Choose Open a PDF in the reading room, or use Open With or Share from Files. If a file is stored in iCloud, download it first. English text recognition runs locally on scanned pages. Check diagrams or uncertain wording in Original PDF.

Bookmarks and settings

Drag sideways across a line in Article or the original PDF’s page lens. Open Bookmarks in the reader menu to revisit or remove it. Tap text to resize all titles, captions or paragraphs of that kind. Reopening the same PDF restores its settings on that device.

Restore or cancel a purchase

Open Reader access and privacy in the library and choose Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for checkout. For a monthly plan, open Apple subscriptions to manage renewal. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or billing period ends to avoid the next charge. Lifetime access is charged once.

Report a bug or suggest a feature

Email contact@dayfiles.com. Include your device model, iOS version, Ink & Echo build and the steps you took. You can describe a document issue without attaching your PDF. Send payment and refund requests through Apple’s purchase support.

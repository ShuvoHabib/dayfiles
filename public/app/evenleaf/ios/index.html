Read a PDF as an article

Turn dense pages into a chapter that fits your screen. Tap a paragraph or title to adjust every passage of that kind. Switch to the original PDF at your reading place when you need a diagram or a table.

Keep your place

Drag across a line to bookmark it. Your library keeps your bookmarks, notes and type settings with each PDF on this device.

Listen as you read

Use a device voice or download the optional English narration pack. Evenleaf generates narration on your device.

Privacy Policy for iOS

Human Anatomy 3D: Body Layers
Effective September 7, 2026 · Published by Dayfiles Studio

This educational anatomy app does not require an account and does not ask you to enter health information. Anatomy viewing, search, structure selection, and preferences are processed on your device.

Advertising

The free version uses Google AdMob. Depending on your region and consent choices, Google may process identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and advertising data to deliver, measure, and secure ads. Where required, the app presents privacy choices before requesting ads, and those choices can be revisited from Settings. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Apple purchases

Monthly, Annual, and Lifetime Pro purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple supplies localized pricing, processes payment, and provides signed purchase status used on the device to unlock or restore Pro. Dayfiles Studio does not receive your complete payment-card details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Local preferences remain until you clear app storage or uninstall the app. Apple retains purchase records as needed to provide purchases, restoration, fraud prevention, and legal compliance. The app has no publisher-operated account, so there is no separate app account to delete.

Children and medical information

The app is intended for a general student and adult audience and is not designed specifically for children. It does not request medical records or provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Contact and changes

Privacy questions can be sent through the Dayfiles contact page. This policy may be updated when the app, providers, or legal requirements change.
